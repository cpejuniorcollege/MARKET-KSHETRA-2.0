ABOUT

MARKET KHETRA

Market Kshetra is a student-driven commerce experience that brings classroom learning into the real world. It gives students an opportunity to transform their ideas into working stalls, products and experiences.

From planning and budgeting to pricing, marketing, sales and customer interaction, students get to experience different aspects of business through hands-on participation.

More than just an exhibition, Market Kshetra is a platform for creativity, entrepreneurship, teamwork, communication and decision-making. Every stall represents an idea brought to life by students.
